you, which includes the $0.45 card fee.

On a **partial refund**, the refund is split in proportion between your share and our fee.
Example: on a $100 sale with a $10 fee, a $50 refund is split $45 from your share and $5 from our
fee, so your part of the refund is $45. Card fees that Stripe doesn't return are handled the same
way as on a full refund. We never charge you twice for the same refunded amount.
