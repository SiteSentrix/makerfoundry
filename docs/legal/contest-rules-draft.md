# MakerFoundry contests (issue #33): a starting point for each contest's rules

**Status:** 2026-10-30. A draft for the owner, not approved. Legal wording, prizes and money are
the owner's (docs/start-here.md); a lawyer's read is advised before the first contest with a prize,
since prize contests have rules of their own in many places (eligibility, "no purchase
necessary", taxes on prizes). A contest opens only with rules text (at `/admin/contests`), so
nothing runs until the owner writes it. Each `[OWNER: …]` is a choice to make.

> **[Contest title]: rules**
>
> - **Who may enter.** Anyone with a MakerFoundry account [OWNER: age, countries, staff
>   excluded?]. Entering is free; nothing has to be bought.
> - **What to enter.** Up to 3 of your own published 3D models on the theme "[theme]", entered
>   before [closing date, UTC]. You confirm you made each one; remixes count only if their
>   licences allow it and they name their parents. Entries must follow MakerFoundry's Terms;
>   models taken down are removed from the contest.
> - **Judging.** [By MakerFoundry's team, on [OWNER: criteria]] / [By members' votes: one vote
>   per account, not for your own entry; MakerFoundry may discount votes it finds to be fraud].
>   MakerFoundry announces up to three places after the contest closes; its decision is final.
> - **Prizes.** [OWNER: what, how delivered, by when; no cash alternative?; taxes are the
>   winner's]. If a winner can't be reached within [OWNER: 14] days, the prize may go to the next
>   place.
> - **Your model stays yours.** Entering gives MakerFoundry no rights beyond those in the Terms;
>   winners' models and maker names may be shown on MakerFoundry [OWNER: and its social pages
>   only with the showcase consent, Terms § 6].
> - **Withdrawing.** You can withdraw an entry until the contest closes.
> - **Changes.** MakerFoundry may cancel or change a contest if it can't run fairly, and says so
>   on the contest's page.
