# Privacy Policy: MakerFoundry

> **DRAFT for the owner, 2026-10-19. Not reviewed by a lawyer. Not published.**

Effective date: [OWNER: date]

**The short version.** You can browse and download free designs without telling us anything. With
a Foundry Account, we keep what the site needs: your account's number and email, your sign-ins,
your maker profile, and the designs and photos you upload. Your email is never shown to other
people. Photos are saved again without their hidden details, such as where they were taken. We
don't sell your data, show ads or track you.

## 1. Who we are

This policy explains how Bruce Wattendorf, doing business as SiteSentrix ("SiteSentrix", "we")
handles personal information on MakerFoundry (makerfoundry.app). Your **Foundry Account** (email,
password, Foundry credits) is run by the StitchFoundry cloud service, and StitchFoundry's Privacy
Policy (stitchfoundry.app/privacy) covers it. This policy covers what MakerFoundry itself keeps.

## 2. What we collect and why

**Purchases and sales.** For each purchase we keep what was bought, by whom, from whom, when, the
price, our fee, the card fee and Stripe's references for the payment, to deliver the buyer's
library, show the seller their sales, and for our accounts: kept 7 years, for tax records. For
sellers we keep the reference of their Stripe account and whether Stripe says it can take
payments. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us; sellers' bank and identity details
are held by Stripe, not by us.

**Agreements.** When you sign up, publish or set up selling, we keep which version of these
policies and terms you agreed to, and when: as long as your account, and for 7 years after a
sale.

**What we never get:** your password (the account service checks it), your card number, and who
downloaded which free design.

## 3. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. Only the
companies that run parts of MakerFoundry for us receive it:

| Company | What it does for us | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| SiteSentrix's StitchFoundry cloud service | Runs Foundry Accounts | Your email and password when you sign up or in; requests to check your sign-in |
| Railway | Hosts the site and its database in the United States | Everything in section 2 except files and photos |
| Cloudflare | Stores the design files and photos (R2), and the site's domain | Design files and photos; Cloudflare also passes visits to the site |
| Stripe | Takes payments and pays sellers | What you give on Stripe's pages; for a purchase, the design's title and price; for a seller, their account number on MakerFoundry |

Each handles data under its own privacy policy: railway.com/legal/privacy,
cloudflare.com/privacypolicy and stripe.com/privacy.

**What's public.** Published designs (files, photos, previews, descriptions) and maker profiles are
public: anyone can see and download them. Drafts are seen only by you, and by our admins if a
design is reported.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to answer a valid copyright notice, to
protect our users or the site from fraud or abuse, or to a buyer if the business is sold, who
must keep this policy's promises.

## 4. Cookies, tracking and advertising

MakerFoundry uses **one cookie**: the sign-in cookie, set only when you sign in, needed to keep
you signed in, readable only by makerfoundry.app over HTTPS. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no
advertising, no third-party scripts.

## 5. Security

- Everything travels over encrypted HTTPS.
- The sign-in cookie can't be read by scripts and is kept by us only as a scrambled hash; the
  account service's sign-in for the same session never reaches your browser.
- When you publish from StitchFoundry or another Foundry app, the app sends your Foundry Account
  sign-in with the design; we check it with the account service for that one request and never
  keep it.
- Files and photos are stored privately under random names; downloads use links that work for a
  few minutes.
- Uploaded files are checked before they're kept, and photos are rebuilt from scratch.
- The database is on Railway's private network, not reachable from the internet, and backed up.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the
authorities as the law requires.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- **See your data.** Your account page, your designs and your maker profile show what we keep.
  Ask at [OWNER: support@makerfoundry.app] for a full copy.
- **Correct it.** Change your maker profile and your designs' details at any time.
- **Delete it.** Delete your drafts, unpublish or delete published designs, and close your account
  (as StitchFoundry's Terms describe). We delete everything except records the law asks us to
  keep, and records of copyright notices and takedowns.

We answer requests within 30 days and never charge for them. We may need to confirm the request
comes from your account's email.

**California** residents have these rights under the CCPA, plus the right not to be treated
differently for using them. We don't sell or "share" personal information for advertising as the
CCPA defines it.

**Europe and the UK.** If you live there, the GDPR gives you these rights, plus the rights to
object, to limit use, to move your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Our
legal bases: running the service you signed up for (account, designs, profile); legal duties
(copyright notices, payment records); and our legitimate interest in keeping the site safe (rate
limits, error logs, reports). Your data is processed in the United States.

## 7. Children, changes and contact

**Children.** Accounts are for adults 18 and over (or the age of majority where you live, if
older). We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child has
made an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

**Changes.** If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you on the site or by email
at least 14 days before it applies. We'll never start using data you already gave us in a new way
without asking you first.
